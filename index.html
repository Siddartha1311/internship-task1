About Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of eleven players. It is one of the most popular sports in the world, combining skill, strategy, teamwork, and endurance across test, ODI, and T20 formats. The best players are remembered for match-winning batting, fast bowling, and exceptional leadership.

Best Cricket Insight: Cricket rewards focus, patience, and smart decision-making; top players like MS Dhoni,Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma are celebrated for their consistency, game-changing moments, and passion for the sport.
